Install the app

One tap. It goes on your home screen like any other app.

Install on Android

Takes 10 seconds in Chrome.

  1. Tap the menu at the top-right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. Open MedH from your home screen.

Samsung Internet: tap ☰ menu → Add page to → Home screen.

Install on iPhone

Apple lets you add it from Safari in 3 taps.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  3. Tap Add. Open MedH from your home screen.

Is it safe? Why not the Play Store?

It installs straight from our own website (a “web app”), so there is no APK file to download and no “unknown sources” warning. It can only do what a website can — it asks before using the camera or sending reminders.

Is my data stored on the phone?

Your records are saved privately in your account, not inside the app. Log in on any phone or laptop and everything is there. Losing the phone doesn’t lose your history.

How do I update it?

You don’t need to. Every time you open it with internet, it loads the newest version.

Does it work without internet?

The app opens without internet, but reading reports with AI and loading your records needs a connection.

How do I remove it?

Long-press the MedH icon and choose Uninstall or Remove, like any app. Your account and records stay safe.
